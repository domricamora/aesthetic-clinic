## Scope and mode

Public home page of the Aesthetic Clinic showcase (resources/js/pages/home.tsx) and, by inheritance, the site layout and the other public pages. Mode: Persuade. Audience: Manila patients deciding to book, and clinic owners evaluating the platform built by recursive-frog.com. Action: book a consultation (patients); explore the admin (owners).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The clinic as a calm, warm treatment room you can step into: the owner's mockup (blush, rose clay, classical serif, soft cards over a full-bleed treatment photo), made production-grade. Refuses the category default of a centred stock hero over three icon cards and a pastel gradient.

OWN-WORLD: Rose-tinted canvas #FBF6F3, blush fields #F2E3DD, rose-clay action #A4604F (white text 4.8:1), clay ink links #8E4E3F, cocoa ink #3B2A28, pale rose-gold accent #E6C2B3 used only on cocoa. Gilda Display (one weight) for display and figures, Geist for text. Soft 10px corners (owner decision for this project), white soft cards with cocoa-tinted long shadows, thin hairlines, full-width bands, no eyebrow labels.

STORY: The visitor sees a real Asian clinic at work, understands in one line that a doctor plans the result, and books in under a minute without an account; three signature treatments with prices answer "what and how much"; proof follows (doctor-first promise, reviews, illustrative before/after); the platform band credits recursive-frog.com and invites owners into the live admin.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Slim cocoa showcase strip (platform credit, "Explore the admin"), sticky ivory header with the aesthetic-clinic wordmark, centred nav and a clay "Book now". Below, a full-bleed looping clinic film (Asian patients) at ~88dvh with a cocoa wash from the left; at left, a two-line Gilda headline "Natural results, planned by your doctor.", a 20-word subline and two actions (clay "Book a consultation", outline "View treatments"). The three signature cards rise over the hero's bottom edge, the middle one higher with the strongest shadow. Signature interaction: the hero copy settles in line by line (12px rise, 60ms stagger, 280ms ease-out; fade only under reduced motion); cards lift on hover on pointer devices.

FORM: Brief-pinned world from the owner's mockup (no concept roll was run because the brief pinned the world); code-led, no image generation available. Seed key: none (pinned brief).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
